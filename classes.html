YOUR FIRST VISIT

Your First Class, Made Simple

Every motherhood journey is different. We want you to feel comfortable, supported, and confident from the moment you walk through our doors.

A few simple preparations can help you settle in, connect with your instructor, and enjoy a practice that feels right for you.

Wear Comfortable Clothing

Choose soft, comfortable clothing that allows you to move freely throughout your session.

Bring Your Essentials

Bring a water bottle and any personal items that help you feel comfortable during class.

Arrive a Little Early

Give yourself time to settle in, explore the space, and speak with your instructor.

Share Your Needs

Let your instructor know about your comfort level, concerns, and any movement limitations.

HERE TO HELP

A Few Things You May Be Wondering

Starting something new can bring questions. Find helpful answers about our classes, your first visit, and how to choose a practice that suits you.

01 Which class is right for me?

The right class depends on your stage of motherhood, comfort, and personal goals. Explore our class options or contact our team for guidance on finding a suitable starting point.

02 Do I need previous fitness experience?

Previous experience may not be necessary for beginner-friendly classes. Let our team know about your experience and needs so we can help you explore an appropriate option.

03 Can I exercise during pregnancy?

Exercise suitability varies from person to person. Consult your healthcare professional before starting or changing an exercise routine, and discuss any relevant guidance with your instructor.

04 What should I bring to class?

Wear comfortable clothing and bring a water bottle along with any personal essentials you may need. Contact us beforehand if you would like to check what equipment is provided.

05 How do I book my first session?

Visit our contact page to get in touch with our team. We can help you discuss your needs, explore suitable classes, and understand the next steps for booking.
