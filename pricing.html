YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

A Little More Clarity for Your Journey

Choosing the right fitness plan is a personal decision. Find answers to common questions about our memberships, class packages, and studio experience.

Still have questions?

We're happy to help you find a plan that feels right for you.

Contact Our Team
What is included in each membership?

Each membership offers a set number of fitness sessions, with options designed around your needs. Please review the membership details above for session counts, booking options, and available guidance.

Can I join during any stage of pregnancy?

Your suitability for exercise depends on your individual circumstances and stage of pregnancy. Speak with your healthcare professional before starting, and let our team know your needs so we can discuss appropriate class options.

Do I need prior fitness experience?

You don't need to be an experienced fitness enthusiast to enquire about our classes. Tell us about your experience and individual needs so we can help you explore a suitable starting point.

Which package is suitable for postnatal recovery?

Our Postnatal Recovery package is one option to explore. The right choice depends on your recovery, individual needs, and healthcare professional's advice. Contact our team to discuss your options before booking.

Can I change or reschedule my classes?

Rescheduling depends on the studio's booking policy and class availability. Please contact our team before booking to confirm the applicable cancellation and rescheduling terms.

What should I bring to my first session?

Comfortable clothing, water, and any personal items you need are a good starting point. Contact us before your first visit to confirm whether your selected class requires anything additional.

Can I attend classes with my baby?

Our Mom & Baby Bonding package is designed around shared activities. Please contact us to confirm the current class schedule, age suitability, and whether your baby can accompany you.

How do I book a class or membership?

Browse the available memberships and packages on this page, then contact our team to discuss your preferred option, class availability, and booking arrangements.
